## Toolchain and isolation

The merge baseline is `08a02ad7f`; the upstream target is `15871f904` (v0.2.22). ## Performance comparisons / 性能对比

Three alternating rounds used the same synthetic bodies and Go toolchain, with 500 ms per initial benchmark. Full results, including slower early experiments, are retained in [retry and body benchmarks](v0.2.22-retry-benchmarks.md). The validation experiment replacing `json.Valid` with `gjson.ValidBytes` was reverted; the final source retains the conservative regex-marker gate and differential correctness tests.

`OpenAIResponsesRetryPreparation` improved by approximately 35–52% in ns/op, 65–67% in B/op and 49–52% in allocs/op for 64 KiB, 1 MiB and 8 MiB bodies across 1/5/15 attempts. Initial transform/schema timings had substantial variation and slower runs. The final controlled comparison fixed Windows CPU affinity to `0x55`, kept `GOMAXPROCS=4`, used three seconds per benchmark, and reversed execution order in round two. The representative 8 MiB image-schema case changed −3.1% and 8 MiB × 15 transform attempts −0.4% in median elapsed time, with effectively unchanged allocations. The earlier regressions did not recur in these controlled cases; all earlier values remain in the report.

[Scheduler and catalog results](v0.2.22-cache-benchmarks.md) include three complete rounds. At 2,000 accounts / 100 shared groups, median scheduler read time decreased 87.0%, B/op 88.0%, allocs/op 99.4%, and fixture p95 50.8%. Retained decoded memory increased 3.6% (about 1.66 MB) from the bounded LastUsed side cache. Catalog rebuild and cached-304 timings stayed within observed variation (+0.4% / −2.1%).

重试准备、共享缓存、目录及大正文复测分别报告，未将本地 helper 的耗时变化换算成生产 CPU 百分比。新增的 Schema 校验差分用例及最终 race 均通过。

The benchmark exercises preparation and transformation helpers only; it is not a production RSS or p95 measurement. No production load test was run.
